Key Capabilities

Study & Routine Timetable

Interactive daily schedule and weekly horizontal timeline. Fast subject filter chips, date jump navigation, priority tagging, and automatic collision warnings when sessions overlap.

Strong & Exact Alarms

High-priority offline reminders configured with Android exact alarms. Survives phone restarts, force-closes, and airplane mode with direct actions to start, snooze, or mark complete.

⏱️

Focus & Pomodoro Timer

Distraction-free countdown timer with 25m, 45m, and 60m presets. Link tasks directly to your study timer to automatically mark tasks completed when your deep work session ends.

No servers, no telemetry, no Google account requirement. All data lives in a local SQLite database on your device with clean one-tap CSV export whenever you need it.

Simple Setup

How to Install Flowra APK

Follow these quick steps to install Flowra directly on any Android device running Android 8.0 or newer.

01
📥

1. Download the APK

Download the latest release APK above using Chrome or your device browser. Android will save the file to your Downloads folder.

02
⚙️

2. Allow Unknown Apps

When opening the APK, Android may display "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps". Tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source".

03
✅

3. Tap Install & Open

Return to the installer, tap Install, and launch Flowra. Grant notification & exact alarm permissions when prompted for reliable reminders.

🔒 How to Verify SHA-256 Checksum

To guarantee that your downloaded APK has not been modified or corrupted, verify its SHA-256 checksum against the official hash published in the release notes above.

On Windows (PowerShell):

Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 .\Flowra-v1.0.0.apk

On macOS / Linux:

sha256sum Flowra-v1.0.0.apk

Android 13 and 14 require explicit permissions (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` and `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) for alarm-style reminders. Exact alarm permissions ensure Flowra fires your scheduled routines at the exact minute rather than being batched or delayed by aggressive battery-saving modes.
